Home/Respite & carer support

Respite & carer support

Short Term Accommodation (STA) and respite care that gives participants a safe, supportive home away from home — and gives families and carers time to rest.

Get in touch1300 853 095

A safe, supportive home away from home

At Rehoboth, our Short Term Accommodation (STA) and respite care services provide a safe, supportive and comfortable home away from home. Whether you are taking a short break, exploring independent living or developing new life skills, our respite homes give you the space and support to recharge, grow and connect.

Short term stays also help families and carers take time to rest, knowing their loved one is receiving quality care in a warm and welcoming environment.

Why families choose Rehoboth respite

  • Flexibility. We understand that life can change unexpectedly. Our short term stays are flexible and can be tailored to suit your needs, whether it's a few days or a few weeks.
  • Comfort and care. Our homes are designed to feel warm and welcoming, offering a balance of comfort, privacy and professional support.
  • Skill development. Every stay is an opportunity to build confidence and independence through daily activities and community participation.
  • Peace of mind for families. Families can rest easy knowing their loved one is supported by trained, caring staff in a safe environment.
  • Community connection. We encourage engagement and inclusion, helping participants take part in local activities, make friends and enjoy new experiences.

What a stay looks like

Respite care at Rehoboth is more than just accommodation — it's an opportunity to relax, learn and thrive in a supportive setting that feels like home. During a stay, participants are supported by trained, caring staff and encouraged to join in daily activities and community life, with the comfort and privacy of their own space to come back to.

You can read more about our short term accommodation on our accommodation page.

Flexible stays, tailored to you

Every family's situation is different, so our short term stays are flexible and can be tailored to suit your needs — whether it's a few days or a few weeks. Each stay offers a balance of comfort, privacy and professional support in a home that feels warm and welcoming.

To talk about availability, call us on 1300 853 095 or see what's currently open.

View current vacancies

Support for carers

Caring for a loved one is rewarding, but every carer needs time to rest and recharge. A respite stay lets you take that break knowing your loved one is in a warm, welcoming home with quality care.

For wider carer support and resources, Carer Gateway is a helpful starting point. And if you'd like to talk through how respite could work for your family, get in touch with our team — we're here to help.
