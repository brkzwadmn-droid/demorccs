About Rehoboth

Who we are

Rehoboth was founded from a deep understanding of what it means to care for someone who needs support. Our long-term vision is to provide exceptional, tailored at-home care that meets each participant's needs and empowers them to live as their best selves.

Our strength is a responsive, person-centred approach to care. We support people living in private and shared NDIS housing across New South Wales, Queensland, the Northern Territory and Victoria, and we pride ourselves on friendly, approachable customer service. Our participants choose to live in our supported homes long term because they feel safe, feel at home, and are supported to live their lives on their own terms.

Rehoboth Comprehensive Care Services is operated by Community Regain Pty Ltd (ABN 12 657 939 896) and is an NDIS registered provider, provider number 4050134271.

About Rehoboth

Our vision: reimagining support

We created Rehoboth to redefine what "home" means for people with disability. A home should be more than just accessible, it should be a place where people feel safe, valued and free to live life their way.

We believe everyone deserves more than just shelter. People with disability have the right to thrive: to experience joy, independence and genuine choice in where and how they live. Our supported homes are built around each person's goals, personality and lifestyle, creating environments that encourage growth, connection and belonging, where participants can express themselves fully and live as their most authentic selves.

Every aspect of what we do is guided by person-centred active support, ensuring that each person is truly at the heart of their home.

Our person-centred approach

We place each person at the heart of everything we do, focusing on abilities, strengths and aspirations rather than limitations. Every support plan is personalised to reflect each participant's goals, preferences and unique circumstances, and is built on genuine listening, respect and collaboration.

Community
